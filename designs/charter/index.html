Articles of purpose

What this company has bound itself to do

  1. Article IWhat you keep here is yours.

    You can always get it out in open or documented formats. It's never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models without your permission.

  2. Article IIThe software works for you.

    It keeps working for the people using it, with your data on your own devices wherever a product's design allows.

  3. Article IIIThe source stays open.

    Applications are published under open-source licenses, and the code stays available if the company stops working on a product.

  4. Article IVNo sudden endings.

    You get reasonable notice before a product or service you rely on ends, and a way to take your data with you.

Annual report
