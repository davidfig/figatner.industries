Four promises

  1. What you keep is yoursYou can always get it out in open or documented formats. It's never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models without your permission.
  2. It works for youThe software keeps working for the people using it, with your data on your own devices wherever a product's design allows.
  3. The source stays openApplications are published under open-source licenses, and the code stays available if the company stops working on a product.
  4. No sudden endingsYou get reasonable notice before a product or service you rely on ends, and a way to take your data with you.
